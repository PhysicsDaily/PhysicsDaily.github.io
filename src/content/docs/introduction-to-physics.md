Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion, and the forces that connect them. It gives us a
way to describe the world precisely, test ideas against evidence, and predict what will happen
next.

Mechanics is the first branch we will study. It begins with the language we need to describe
position, motion, and forces.

Continue to [Chapter 1: Vectors](./mechanics/chapter-1-vectors/) to learn that language.
